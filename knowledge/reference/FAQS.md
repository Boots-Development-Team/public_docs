## What are the requirements to pass BGC?
 The BGC requirements depend on the division you are applying for. Each division has its own set of requirements that must be met in order to pass the BGC. These requirements may include age, clean history, and other qualifications. It is important to review the specific requirements for the division you are interested in before applying. Each division has its own criteria. For further information, open a DST ticket.

## How do I join a dvision?
 To join a division, you must first meet the requirements for that division. Once you have met the requirements, you can attend a tryout or selection process for that division.

## How do rank up in the USM team (enlisted)?
 To rank up in the USM team as an enlisted member, you must earn XP. You may do so by attending ETs o AETs based on your rank. You may also earn XP by being in-game, though this is not the most efficient way to earn XP.

## How do I rank up in the USM team (officer)?
 To rank up in the USM team as an officer, you must attend OTs.

## How long does it usually take to be ranked upon passing an OT?
 The usual time it takes to be ranked upon passing an OT is 24-48 hours. If that time has passed and you have not been ranked, please open a general support ticket for assistance.

## It's been more than 48 hours since I passed a tryout/selection and I haven't received any updates, what should I do?
 If it has been more than 48 hours since you passed a tryout/selection and you haven't received any updates, you most likely failed your BGC. You may open a DST ticket to inquire about your BGC results.

## I'm a guest in {any} division (DOESNT INCLUDE USM, USM IS NOT A DIVISION ITS THE MAIN TEAM), how do I rank up?
 If you are a guest in any division, you will not be able to rank up. You must first become a member of the division before you can rank up. Buying the team doesn't grant membership or the ability to rank up. You must first meet the requirements for the division and be accepted as a member before you can rank up.

## How do I join the Staff?
 Staff members are either handpicked or selected after passing an interview. Do not ask to join the staff; doing so may lead to a permanent blacklist from staff opportunities.

## HOW CAN I REACH THE RANK [O7]?
1. Purchase the Gamepass: Brigadier General Rank.
2. Reach the rank of [07] in any division.

## HOW CAN I GET THE CELEBRITY RANK?
1. Retire as an [09 - 010] in any division, with at least 3 months of service.
2. Staff High Command or higher, with at least 3 months of service.

## CAN I GET MY GAMEPASS REFUNDED?
No, we cannot refund any Gamepasses.

## CAN I APPEAL TIME BANS?
No, time bans cannot be appealed. Only permanent or indefinite bans can be appealed, and only after one month from the date of the ban.

## CAN I TRANSFER MY GAMEPASS TO ANOTHER ACCOUNT?
No, Gamepasses cannot be transferred between accounts.
