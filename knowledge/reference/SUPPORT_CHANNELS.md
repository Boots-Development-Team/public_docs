## Ticket classification
 Tickets are classified, meaning that users are NOT allowed to distribute, leak or share any information from tickets outside of the ticket itself unless the ticket is declassified by Senior Management or above. This includes, but is not limited to, screenshots, recordings, or any other form of media. Users who violate this rule may face disciplinary action, including but not limited to, being banned from the server or even being blacklisted from CUSA, depending on the severity of the violation.
 
## General support inquiries
 For general support inquiries, point users to the support channel (<#1024870590617767996>) in the main CUSA server and open a ticket based on their inquiry. The Staff Team will then assist them with their inquiry (tell them NOT to ping staff members and be patient). If the inquiry is related to a specific division, the Staff Team will direct them to the appropriate Divisional Support Team (DST) server for further assistance.

## User and Staff reports
 For user and staff reports, point users to the support channel (<#1024870590617767996>) in the main CUSA server and open a ticket based on their report. The Staff Team will then assist them with their report (tell them NOT to ping staff members and be patient). For Staff reports, this will be handled by Senior Management. 

## Divisional support
 For divisional support, point users to the support channel (<#1024870590617767996>) in the main CUSA server so they can join/go to the DST (Divisional Support Team) server. The DST will then assist them with their inquiry.

## Appeals
 For appeals regarding in-game or main CUSA server bans, point users to the support channel (<#1024870590617767996>) in the main CUSA server so they can join/go to the Appeals server. The Appeals team will then assist them with their appeal.

## HICOM contact or inquiries escalation
 For HICOM contact or inquiries escalation, point users to the CoC (Chain of Command) channel (<#986649408974237696>) in the main CUSA server.
