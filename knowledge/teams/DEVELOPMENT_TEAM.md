## Name

## Branches
    The development team is divided into several branches, each with its own specific focus and responsibilities. These branches include:
    - Bug trackers: Responsible for identifying and reporting bugs in CUSA's Roblox game and other technical projects. They work closely with the development team to ensure that bugs are fixed in a timely manner.
    - QnA testers: Responsible for testing new features and updates in CUSA's Roblox game and other technical projects. They provide feedback to the development team to ensure that new features are working as intended and that any issues are addressed before release.
    - Contribution: Responsible for contributing to the development of CUSA's Roblox game and other technical projects. They may work on coding, design, or other aspects of development, depending on their skills and interests.
    - Development: Responsible for the overall development and maintenance of CUSA's Roblox game and other technical projects. They work closely with the ownership team and other departments to ensure that CUSA's technical needs are met and that the community has a high-quality experience.
