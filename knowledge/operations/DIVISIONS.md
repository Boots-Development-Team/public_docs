## Divisional rules
Within your chosen branch, you may hold one Main Division and one Sub-Division. Holding two Sub-Divisions or two Main Divisions within the same branch requires approval.

## Units
Maximum of 1 unit allowed. You may hold a High Command (HICOM) position in 1 Unit.

## Branch
You may only be a member of one DEPNAV Branch at a time (United States Marine Corps or United States Navy).
Current branches are Department of Army, Department of Navy, Department of Homeland Security.

## Special permission
With permission from DOJ HICOM: You may hold a HICOM position in DOJ and one other division unless you are DSOJ+.

## Restrictions
Members of either chamber of Congress (the House of Representatives or the Senate) may not simultaneously serve under the Department of Justice or the Executive Office of the President.
You can't be in more than one intelligence agency without approval, regardless the branch

## Cross-Branch restrictions
Rangers and Raiders Regiment members cannot join each other’s regiments.
