## CUSA in-game USM formations

 Wedge: Wedge formation consists of a lead person positioned at the front, with the remaining members arranged diagonally behind them on both sides, forming a V-shaped formation. Most commonly used for graduation pictures and guarding.

 Shoulder to Shoulder (STS): One or more attendee(s) stand shoulder to shoulder, facing the host in rank order. This simple and effective setup is one of the most commonly used formations in the military. It allows the host to quickly observe, address, or organize the group.

 Double Column: Double Column consists of attendees standing shoulder to shoulder in two side-by-side lines, directly in front of the host, facing the host. The highest-ranking members stand in the back, while lower ranks are positioned up front. This formation is ideal for managing large groups during training or organized events.

 Single Column: This formation consists of two or more people standing side by side, aligned shoulder to shoulder. It is commonly used to provide maximum visibility and control, making it easier for a leader or host to observe and organize their group.

 Diamond Formation: The Diamond formation is commonly used by guarding divisions to protect high profile individuals such as the President. It consists of 4 individuals positioned in a diamond shaped formation to ensure coverage on the closest areas to the VIP.

 Orb formation: Orb formation is used to protect the person in the center and sense danger or a threat from every single direction. This formation is the safest out of all due to its 360 degree coverage.

 Box formation: Box formation is used to protect the high value individuals in the center of the formation. It is one of the President’s safeguard and it covers all four directions with 4 guards who can respond to any threat from any side.
