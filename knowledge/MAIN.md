## About CUSA

CUSA is a Roblox roleplay community. ("Clockwork's United States Army") It is a military-based roleplay group that focuses on providing a realistic and immersive experience for its members. CUSA has a structured hierarchy, with ranks and positions that members can achieve through dedication and participation in the community. https://www.roblox.com/games/6022383883/UPD-Military-Roleplay
